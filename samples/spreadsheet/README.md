# Spreadsheet agent samples

In Chat, choose **Spreadsheet Analyst**, attach one CSV below, and ask one of its questions.
These examples are synthetic.

## `sales.csv`

- “What was total revenue by region, highest first?” Expected top region: North, 2,350.
- “Which product sold the most units?” Expected product: Beacon, 69 units.
- “Show North sales with revenue greater than 500.” Expected three rows.

## `budget-vs-actual.csv`

- “Show Research's approved budget and actual spend.” Expected: Approved 140,000; Actual 152,000.

## `inventory.csv`
