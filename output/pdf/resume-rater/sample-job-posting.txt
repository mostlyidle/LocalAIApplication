Senior Backend Engineer
Northstar Software | Remote in Canada

Northstar Software builds workflow tools for independent logistics providers. We are looking for a backend engineer to build and operate reliable services that support dispatch, billing, and customer account workflows.

What you will do
- Design, build, and maintain Python backend services and versioned REST APIs.
- Work with product and frontend engineers to turn customer needs into clear service contracts.
- Model data in PostgreSQL, write maintainable SQL, and investigate query performance.
- Keep production services dependable through automated tests, observability, incident response, and clear runbooks.
- Improve asynchronous workflows for scheduled and event-driven work.
- Review designs and code, share technical context, and help teammates grow through collaboration and mentorship.

What we are looking for
- Demonstrated experience delivering and supporting Python services in production.
- Practical API design experience, including validation, error handling, and backward-compatible changes.
- Strong working knowledge of PostgreSQL and SQL query behavior.
- Experience improving service reliability with testing and operational practices.
- Clear written communication and constructive collaboration across roles.

Helpful experience
- FastAPI or a similar Python API framework.
- Background jobs or message queues such as Celery, Redis Queue, SQS, or an equivalent.
- Distributed tracing, service-level objectives, or production incident ownership.
- Containerized deployments and cloud infrastructure.

Please describe your specific contributions, the systems you supported, and measurable outcomes where you can substantiate them. Equivalent experience is welcome; no particular degree or career path is required.
